# Personas

These are working hypotheses, not validated customer definitions.

## New engineer

Needs to understand a company's systems, ownership, conventions, and important context quickly. The strongest immediate pain is not lack of documentation; it is knowing what to read, who to ask, and why a system looks the way it does.

**Desired outcome:** reach useful independence faster without repeatedly interrupting experienced engineers.

## Engineering manager / team lead

Needs visibility into ownership and knowledge concentration and wants onboarding to become less dependent on individual availability.

**Desired outcome:** predictable onboarding and fewer context-transfer bottlenecks.

## Engineering organization

Needs company knowledge to survive employee movement and remain connected to the code and conversations where it originated.

**Desired outcome:** lower knowledge-loss and context-recovery cost.

## Buying hypothesis

The likely buyer and user may differ. This must be validated through customer interviews and usage rather than assumed from the current role model in the application.
