# Product Metrics

Metrics are hypotheses to validate, not vanity targets.

## Primary outcome

**Time-to-context:** time for a new engineer to answer and act on a representative set of questions about an unfamiliar codebase without human escalation.

## Supporting metrics

- Ramp completion and step engagement.
- Percentage of Ramp steps backed by concrete company evidence.
- Questions answered successfully without escalation.
- Time from question to useful answer.
- Answer citation/source usefulness.
- Repeated onboarding questions avoided.
- Weekly active engineers using KMS.
- Retention of teams after initial onboarding.

## Reliability/product quality guardrails

- Tenant isolation failures: zero tolerated.
- Unsupported or fabricated ownership/context claims: zero tolerated.
- Ingestion failures must be observable rather than silently treated as success.

## Validation rule

Do not optimize these metrics before establishing a baseline with real users. The first goal is learning whether KMS measurably reduces context-acquisition work and whether teams care enough to keep using it.
